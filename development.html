Why MRI?

When we decided to tackle healthcare access, we had options such as CT scans, ultrasounds, X-rays. So why MRI? MRI catches what other imaging misses: brain tumors, spinal injuries, torn ligaments, early cancers.

The problem? MRI machines cost millions. They need liquid helium cooling, massive power consumption, and specialized facilities. A rural clinic can't afford one. That's why MRI. Highest impact, widest access gap.

Our Business Model

Why Leasing?

Leasing allows healthcare providers to deploy MRI systems without ownership or large upfront investment. The equipment remains owned by the provider, enabling stable access to advanced imaging while avoiding long-term asset and depreciation risks.

With fixed contract periods and predictable monthly costs, leasing simplifies planning. Installation, maintenance, repairs, and software updates can be bundled—ensuring reliable operation without requiring in-house technical expertise.
